BUBBLES

bubbles is a playful reinterpretation of the address book that allows users to nest and organize their social circles. when combined with the platform’s AI-powered contact importing and information sorting tools, bubbles becomes the ideal address book for the digital age. bubbles is my capstone project for my parsons mps studio class, originally prototyped in figma and now vibe-coded in swift for ios over the course of eight weeks. app launch coming soon!
