GUTCHECK

gutcheck is a chrome extension designed + developed to help users reduce impulse shopping on amazon while providing local shopping alternatives. this was my typography and interaction capstone project at parsons, which was completed over a six week design and development period. as part of our curriculum, we were encourage to develop the product without ai vibe-coding tools, and gutcheck is the result. coming soon to the chrome web store.
